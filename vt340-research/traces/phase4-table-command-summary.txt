VT340 Phase 4 DC323 startup-table command summary
=================================================

Captured from the two CPU2 calls to 17CF between configuration entry 1325
and the mailbox clear at 136D. Times are MAME emulation time.

Each 17CF group submits 18 I/D commands, in this order:

  0180 0181 0183 0188 0189 0184 0185 0186 0187
  018B 018E 018F 0190 0191 0192 0194 0195 0196

The two groups therefore submit 36 I/D commands total. Consecutive commands
are normally 24 us apart. Each group has one 38 us gap between 0189 and 0184,
and each complete group lasts 2.383 ms.

The documented immediate command-spacing requirement is approximately 3 us.
The observed 24 us spacing is already about eight times that minimum; omitted
immediate-ready latency cannot add the approximately 6.9 ms required to put
CPU1 pulse 6 before CPU2's 136D mailbox clear.

The table stream also independently supports the generalized dump repair.
The first table crosses E4 logical 60FF (CPU address E0FF). Raw offset 6000
contains 3A, producing the coherent next command/value record `3A 38 88`.
The naive one-byte rotation would use raw 6100 (`C5`) and break the otherwise
ordered table. E3 logical 30FF is similarly constrained as `80`, producing
the coherent `SJMP 3119` in the zero-configuration routine.
