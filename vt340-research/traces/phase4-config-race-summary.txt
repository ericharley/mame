VT340 Phase 4 E795 control-byte experiment
==========================================

Question
--------

CPU2 reads shared E795 at 1325 immediately after its sixth 1701 rendezvous.
CPU1 writes E795 immediately before it installs the 7FF2 pending sentinel.
Could CPU2 be consuming a stale configuration byte and thereby taking the
wrong path to the 136D checkpoint?

Observed native ordering
------------------------

  8.220509  CPU1 emits pulse 5
  8.220511  pulse 5 releases CPU2's 1322 rendezvous; CPU2 reads E795=00
  8.228779  CPU2 writes checkpoint 7FF2=00
  8.235677  CPU1 emits pulse 6
  8.295750  CPU1 accepts response 6
  8.295768  CPU1 writes its computed E795=01
  8.295773  CPU1 installs pending sentinel 7FF2=10

The CPU2 sequence is therefore launched by the DAT9 rendezvous, not by the
7FF2 value. CPU1's E795 update is also intentionally downstream of CPU2's
current read in the executed ROM order.

Controlled experiment
---------------------

`phase4-config-race-experiment.lua` writes 01 to E795 at CPU2 1325, the exact
decision point, then leaves both processors' PCs and all other state alone.
CPU2 takes its authentic nonzero branch at 1334.

  8.220511  force E795=01 at CPU2 1325
  8.220518  CPU2 enters nonzero path 1334
  8.228431  CPU2 writes checkpoint 00
  8.295423  CPU1 installs pending sentinel 10
  8.769443  CPU2 writes final result 80

Result
------

The nonzero path moves the checkpoint 0.348 ms earlier, increasing the bad
checkpoint-before-sentinel lead. It also skips later work and publishes the
final result about 4.43 seconds earlier, while CPU1 is still trapped in its
checkpoint loop. E795 path selection is therefore not the missing behavior
that can restore sentinel -> checkpoint -> result ordering. The forced value
is diagnostic only and is not enabled in the driver.
